
# FxLink Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026.

## Overview

FxLink is a browser extension that automatically converts copied X/Twitter post URLs into FxTwitter URLs.

## Data Collection

FxLink does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal information to the developer or external servers.

## Clipboard Access

FxLink processes copied X/Twitter post URLs locally in the browser to convert them into FxTwitter URLs.

Clipboard content is not transmitted to external servers by the extension.

## Local Storage

FxLink uses chrome.storage.local exclusively to remember whether automatic link conversion is enabled or disabled.

## Third-Party Services

FxLink generates links to fxtwitter.com. Opening these links is subject to the destination service's own privacy practices.

## User Control

Users can disable automatic conversion through the extension popup or uninstall the extension at any time.
